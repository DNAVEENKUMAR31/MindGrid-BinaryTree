🎮 Game Overview

MindGrid is an intelligent Tic-Tac-Toe experience powered by the Minimax Algorithm. It not only challenges your mind but also visualizes the AI’s decision-making process in real time through a Binary Tree representation.

📜 Rules & Gameplay

  • The game is played on a 3×3 grid between you (X) and AI (O).
  • Players take turns placing their mark in empty cells.
  • The first to align 3 marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins.
  • If all 9 cells are filled without a winner, the game ends in a draw.
  • You can restart anytime using the Restart button.

🧩 How the AI Thinks (Minimax Algorithm)

The AI simulates all possible moves using a Binary Tree structure where each node represents a future game state. Each move is assigned a “score” based on the outcome:

  • 10 - depth → Fast AI win (best score).
  • 0 → Draw or human win.

The AI always chooses the move with the highest score, unless you select a lower difficulty mode where it intentionally makes less optimal choices.

🏆 Scoring System

  • +1 point for each win.
  • 0 for a loss.
  • Draws are tracked separately.
  • Your total score is shown in the scoreboard at the top of the game screen.

⚙️ Difficulty Modes

  • Perfect (Unbeatable): The AI always makes the optimal move.
  • Normal (Balanced): The AI occasionally makes small mistakes.
  • Random (Easy): The AI picks moves randomly.
